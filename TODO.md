- amplicon primer removal limitations (documented in docs/usage.md):
    - single-end read-through: PIMENTO (`--merged`, reverse primers searched in the last 50 bases of the reads, reported if in > 60% of them) does not find the reverse primer in single-end reads shorter than the amplicon, in which it is only in the read-through reads, before the adapter. So only the reverse complement of the forward primer is trimmed by CUTADAPT_READTHROUGH, and the read-through reads keep the reverse complement of the reverse primer, and the adapter and random bases after it, which fastp does not remove in files of less than 10000 reads (`primers_se` in `tests/test_primer_removal.nf.test`: 6 reads read-through trimmed, for 69 read-through reads). The reverse primer cannot be deduced from the region of the forward primer (several reverse primers per region in the PIMENTO library, V3-V4: 805R, 806R, 806BR, 785R, 798R, some binding inside the amplicon of the others). Tried and dropped (git stash "primer trimming after QC, mergepairs_wgs (abandoned)"): running fastp before PIMENTO and cutadapt, which finds the reverse primer only if most single-end reads read through and fastp detects the adapter, but needs the pairs of amplicon runs not to be merged by fastp (`--shortread_qc_mergepairs` only for WGS) and the analysis-ready amplicon reads published after the trimming
    - libraries ligated in both orientations: each primer must be in > 60% of the reads of a mate, so with 40-60% of the pairs flipped (e.g. 50/50) PIMENTO finds no primer and the run is silently not trimmed (only 25% flipped is tested, `mixed_orientation`). Could warn when no primer is found, or lower `-m`
    - single-end reads in the other orientation (starting with the reverse primer) are not recognised by `--merged`, which searches the reverse primers only at the end of the reads
- fastp WGS preprocessing vs the vortex_knight bbduk one (`qtrim=rl trimq=3 maq=25 ktrim=r k=23 mink=11 hdist=1 ftm=5 tpe tbo`): adapter removal is fine for PE (overlap analysis + `--detect_adapter_for_pe`), but:
    - SE adapter auto-detection works on the first 256K reads only, is skipped for files of less than 10000 reads, and can miss adapters not in the fastp list of known adapters in libraries with few of them: recommend/provide `--shortread_qc_adapterlist`
    - polyG trimming is auto-enabled only if the read headers show a NovaSeq/NextSeq instrument, often not the case for SRA/ENA downloads: consider `--trim_poly_g` by default
    - no quality trimming at the read ends by default (fastp only filters whole reads): consider `--cut_tail` and `--average_qual`
    - `--length_required 15` (`shortread_qc_minlength`) is permissive compared to vortex_knight (75, 45 for functional profiling)
    - `--detect_adapter_for_pe` is passed twice to FASTP_PAIRED (nf-core module and `conf/modules.config`), harmless
